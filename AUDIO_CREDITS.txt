Integration notes
- Samples are embedded in app.js as base64 WAV data: no runtime CDN/network dependency.
- WebAudio decodeAudioData is used once per AudioContext; decoded AudioBuffers are cached.
- Existing synthesized priority cue remains only as a decode/autoplay fallback.
- Procedural music and UI feedback remain WebAudio-generated as before.

M115 mix update (2026-09-25)
- No additional third-party audio file is claimed in this milestone.
- M115 keeps the two CC0 recordings above and improves how they are presented: service-specific pitch/rhythm profiles, directional stereo panning when supported, horn variation, music ducking, and a bounded six-voice vehicle-audio budget.
- Police / ambulance / fire remain based on the recorded emergency source from sound 70936 in this checkpoint; they are differentiated by playback/mix behavior rather than by claiming three separate source recordings.

M116 runtime mix note: emergency approach cues now scale by simulated distance to the stop line; horns are suppressed while an emergency cue is active. No new audio asset or license was added in M116.

M122 Audio 2.0 production mix (2026-09-25)
- No new third-party recording is claimed in this milestone.
- The retained CC0 recorded siren and horn sources above remain the only embedded third-party recordings.
- New brake scrub, bus air-brake texture, road-bed and crash layers are generated locally with WebAudio noise/filter/envelope synthesis and contain no external asset.
- Crash audio is layered (impact noise + low rumble + high transient + tonal body) and road-bed automatically disables on Low render quality.
- Generated audio is bounded to six concurrent transient voices; recorded vehicle voices remain bounded to six.
- Road-bed never initiates AudioContext resume; M112-HF03 iOS/WebKit recovery authority remains unchanged.
